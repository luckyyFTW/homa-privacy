Homa · Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. Homa is a game by Potato Studio (contact: potatostudiogames@gmail.com).

The short version

Homa does not ask for your name, email, location or contacts. Your town is saved on your phone and, so you don't lose it, also on our server under a random guest id. Your steps are counted by your phone and only turned into in-game gems.

What the game uses

  • Your town save (the buildings, residents with made-up names, coins and gems, your pet's name and your town's name) is stored on your phone. A copy is sent to our server (Cloudflare) under a random guest id, to keep it safe and to let friends you share your town code with visit your town (read only).
  • Physical activity (step count): if you allow it, the game reads your phone's step counter to give you gems for walking. The steps are counted on your phone. Only the total number of steps walked in the game is part of your town save. No location is used, ever.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos are used.
  • No account is needed. There is no sign-in with your personal details.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, we don't show personalised ads at this time, and we don't share your data with anyone except our hosting provider (Cloudflare), which stores the town saves for us.

Ads and purchases

Future versions may offer optional in-app purchases (gems) through Google Play, and optional rewarded ads. If ads are added, this policy will be updated first and will list the ad provider.

Children

Homa is meant for players aged 13 and over and is family-friendly. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use "New town" in the game's settings, or uninstall the game, to delete it from your phone. To delete the copy on our server, email potatostudiogames@gmail.com with your town code and we will delete it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
