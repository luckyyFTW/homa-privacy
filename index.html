Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional: you can play without it. If you sign in, Google tells us your account id, email address and first name. We use them only to keep your town linked to you. We never post anything, and we never read your contacts, files or anything else from your Google account.

You can log out from the start screen at any time. Your town stays safe online until you sign in again. Google's own privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Friends

Every town has a short code. Friends who know your username or code can visit your town (just looking, they can't change anything), send you a small gift, and like your town. Only your town is shown to them, never your email or Google account.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game sets little reminders on your phone (crops ready, your daily gift, a storm coming). They are made on your phone; we don't send them from a server. You can switch them off in the game's settings or your phone's settings.

Purchases and ads

Homa offers optional gem packs, special offers and a monthly Gold Pass through Google Play. Payments are handled by Google; we never see your card details.

Homa also has optional video ads: an ad only plays when you tap "watch an ad" to get free gems, never on its own. The ads come from Google AdMob. They are non-personalised and family-safe, and the game does not use your advertising id. To show an ad and count it, AdMob may receive your device's IP address, basic device information and how you interacted with the ad. Google's policy for this is at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Children

Homa is family-friendly and meant for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has shared their details with us, email us and we will delete them.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: use "New town" in the game's settings, or uninstall the game.
  • On our server: email potatostudiogames@gmail.com with your username or town code. We will delete your town, username and Google link within 30 days.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions? Email Potato Studio at potatostudiogames@gmail.com.
